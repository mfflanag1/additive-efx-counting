# Next goods campaign: global admission, not another local selector

Priority set 5 September 2026. This brief prepares the next research run; it does not assert that the theorem below holds or launch a long-running computation.

## Why this is the next target

The August 26 premise audit is the controlling record. The twelve-atom master has 316 exact response relations; its physical totals/minima extension has 679. These are classifications of particular models, not a proof that arbitrary globally selected EFX states enter those models. P10-P15 are not encoded. "Exactly five response edges" was inserted, and the purported compulsory response d->r fails in 363 physical-master relations. A response edge is NOT a transfer edge in a closing allocation.

Four-edge closure is refuted on the tested master. Five-edge closure has no completed universal certificate in the reviewed checkpoint. A successful local five-edge theorem would still need global admission. Consequently, do not spend the next run enlarging a template menu or accumulating more successful census examples.

## Frozen target: ADMIT4

Let I be a finite four-agent positive additive goods instance, initially under the declared nondegeneracy convention (each agent gives distinct values to distinct subsets). Fix an owner priority order. Suppose I has no complete EFX allocation. Prove that I admits a legally constructed witness in a precisely stated physical fixed-U domain D, such that the local closure conclusion over D pulls back to either a complete EFX allocation of I or a strictly improving partial EFX allocation contradicting the selected global maximality.

Start with D equal to the literal physical-minimum master associated with `build_rank_three_master` at commit `5d13f17e8258f2d9386467838d48c482cf72b071`, including its full base/support layouts and packet inequalities, but without restricting the response relation to the two named forms. Freeze the actual source predicate in the new work branch before proving anything; the two reference documents alone are not an executable specification. If its value box, normalization or layout cannot be derived, prove a justified enlarged-domain admission theorem and state which existing closure results no longer apply.

ADMIT4 is a proposed reduction, NOT an established equivalence and NOT the assertion that every partial EFX state is represented. An existential choice of a legal witness suffices, but all existential choices, their order and their constraints must be explicit. Defining D as "whatever states arise" is not a useful outcome: D must have independently usable inequalities and an explicit closure interface.

## Proof obligations, in order

### A0. Joint selection of the global starting state

Define the finite set of partial EFX allocations of the ORIGINAL goods and the exact lexicographic objective over that set. Distinguish a global maximizer from a maximizer restricted to one leftover good. BCFF supplies an almost-complete allocation; it does not, without a separate argument, supply a global lexicographic maximizer having exactly one leftover good.

Prove the joint property needed by the later argument, or replace it with a selection principle whose legally improving exits really contradict its own maximality. List every discarded case and the theorem that closes it. Do not use the two-nonsingleton exclusion until its one-charity/maximality hypotheses have been obtained. If this joint-selection lemma fails, freeze an exact physical counterexample to that lemma and stop the downstream admission proof at the failure.

### A1. Physical role extraction without deleting goods

Give a labeled partition of the original goods into the named roles and charity, with every intermediate support state and signed transfer recorded. Role totals and observer minima must be computed from those same goods; arbitrary marginal numbers are not a physical instance. Specify empty and singleton roles, common cardinalities, and how minima are realized. Do not identify a multi-good role with a single atom merely because its total agrees.

Derive P1-P9, one inequality at a time, from actual base EFX, chosen legal operations and their failure conditions. Explain all per-agent rescalings and prove any bound such as v<16 after base normalization; otherwise remove the bound and reopen dependent closure claims. Include charity in the exhaustive accounting. Provide actual derivations for the root-facing branch and every alternative branch, not a WLOG label.

### A2. Coverage, saturation and choice independence

For P10-P15 state whether each is derived, unnecessary for a direct complete-allocation closure, or still open. Define PI-freeness, response saturation, desired cores, ledgers and reachability operationally. A failed candidate must imply a legally available next state, not merely a new response label.

Cover ALL response relations admitted by the derived domain. Do not assume exactly five response edges, d->r for physical packets, form 2, a four-edge transfer bound, fixed packet cardinality, or a fixed moved-good radius. If restricting any of these, prove the restriction from the physical premises. A larger master can simplify this obligation, but its local closure must then be proved for that larger master.

### A3. Pullback and continuation interface

For every closing allocation allowed by D, prove that assigning the corresponding original role unions is complete and satisfies the exact original EFX inequalities. If splitting is permitted, prove common realizability and the inequalities for the actual selected goods, not independent observer endpoints that no common good realizes.

A complete EFX exit need not lexicographically improve the selected partial allocation. A PARTIAL exit must be legal for the same optimization domain, preserve all required charity conditions, and strictly improve the stated potential. If the route uses iteration rather than an immediate contradiction, supply a finite well-founded measure. Do not revive the retired T_L,times floor-reset closure or its unproved successor containment.

## Two-sided attack and falsifiers

First attack A0 by exact enumeration of actual small positive rational games and all their partial allocations. The test oracle must calculate global and one-charity maxima independently, with the priority order explicit. It is legitimate to refute a proposed joint-selection sublemma on a game that itself HAS a complete EFX allocation, provided that sublemma did not assume the contrary. Such a witness does not refute ADMIT4 under its no-complete-EFX antecedent.

For A1-A3, freeze each proposed implication BEFORE searching. Negative results must satisfy every antecedent of that implication, and defeat its full existential choice set if the claim is existential. A failure of one chosen selector or one partition is not a counterexample to admission. A marginal profile satisfying only P1-P9 is not a counterexample to a stronger global-premise theorem.

If an exact positive rational instance truly has NO complete EFX allocation, require unrestricted allocation verification and independent arithmetic/encoding before treating it as an EFX counterexample. Allocation caps and necessary relaxations do not suffice.

## Calibration and stop/go gates

1. Reproduce the existing 316/679 relation boundaries and the missing d->r phenomenon from the frozen definitions, reusing archived certificates where valid. The purpose is specification fidelity, not another census claim.
2. Accept the known four-edge residuals and their five-edge completions. A new admission restriction excluding them needs a genuine global reason.
3. Check the exact theorem/countermodel examples used in the BCFF import audit and reject the retired reset transition for its recorded failure.
4. Before any long search, finish a proof-state table for A0-A3 with the first unproved implication highlighted. If A0 is open, spend the run there; do not start a downstream W5 continuation merely to remain busy.
5. Continue only for a new conventional derivation, a new physical obstruction to a frozen sublemma, or a materially smaller EXACT missing premise. A growing menu or a restated sufficient condition is not this campaign's success criterion.

## Deliverables

- A precise ADMIT4 theorem with every quantifier, definition and dependency, OR an exact obstruction to a named admission sublemma with the remaining weaker target stated.
- A P1-P15 source-to-proof table, including all omitted branches and the exact point where each hypothesis enters.
- A physical witness/transition format, independently checked selector/maximality and pullback tests, rational arithmetic, fail-closed outcomes and reproduction commands.
- A short conventional proof or a hostile-review packet isolating the first genuinely unresolved implication. Bounded checks remain bounded checks.
- The exact remaining bridge: admission alone does not prove W5; W5 alone does not prove admission. Their composition must still cover all admitted states and tie/zero wrappers. Even four-agent existence for arbitrary m would not prove arbitrary-agent additive EFX.
