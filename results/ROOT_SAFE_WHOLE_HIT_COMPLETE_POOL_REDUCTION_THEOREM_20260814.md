# Root safe whole-hit complete-pool reduction theorem

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Status:** cap-free unification of strict, equality, and base-floor
root-held whole hits; exact complete-pool underpayment/equality remains

## Desire-free root input

Let the protected first-priority root hold

\[
 Z_r=D\mathbin{\dot\cup}L,
 \qquad v_r(Z_r)=x+\delta,
 \qquad \delta>0,                                \tag{1}
\]

where `D` is common-safe at the actual canonical allocation, contains the
whole named packet `K`, and belongs to the named protected source/colliding
recipient `t` of the exact transition. No current-desire premise is required.
A protected-floor-safe core qualifies whenever every current assignment pays
its typed floor. The theorem does not assign `D` to an arbitrary owner.

## Direct anchor/complement branches

If `v_r(D)>x`, retain `D` as a safe current root anchor. If `v_r(L)>x`, give
`D` to its named nonroot recipient `t`, keep `L` at the root, and send `Z_t`
to charity. This is the actual protected-source/collision handoff. The root
remains strict, so every later owner may be sacrificed.

At the resulting candidate, the only target containing `K` is the safe
target `D`. A root wall against it yields a safe response worth more than the
new root target and hence more than `x`, giving anchor reselection. A
nonroot wall enters the desire-free nonroot nested-envelope theorem. All
other responses avoid `K`.

Thus the only root-value survivor satisfies

\[
 \delta<v_r(D)<x,
 \qquad \delta<v_r(L)<x.                         \tag{2}
\]

## Complete compatible exterior pool

Define the exact root price

\[
 \pi=v_r(D)-\delta>0.                            \tag{3}
\]

Let `mathfrak R_r` contain every maximal mutually compatible supplier-closed
`K`-free exterior mode after canonical normalization of other retired
support. For any `R in mathfrak R_r`, give `D` to its named nonroot recipient
`t` and `L+R` to the root, let exterior donors keep their residuals, and
place the unused part of `Z_t` in charity.

The root margin is

\[
 v_r(L+R)-x=v_r(R)-\pi.                          \tag{4}
\]

If `v_r(R)>pi`, the root is strict. The same wall localization as above
routes every response meeting `K` to root-anchor reselection or the finite
nonroot envelope descent. No current-desire, recipient-margin, or safety
premise on `R` is used.

## Unified exact survivor

After all strict modes are exhausted, every root-held common-safe whole hit,
regardless of whether it arose from strict capture, equality capture, or a
base-floor/source-inactive core, satisfies

\[
 v_r(R)\le v_r(D)-\delta
 \qquad(R\in\mathfrak R_r).                     \tag{5}
\]

There are only two scalar subfaces:

1. **aggregate underpayment:** every inequality in (5) is strict; or
2. **root equality:** some compatible mode has equality, returning the root
   exactly to base while later donors may lose.

The source label and protected-floor debt no longer form an independent
whole-hit obstruction. They matter only in the equality-priority audit or in
a response not yet converted to a common-safe core.

## Smallest missing operation

The remaining direct operation must do at least one of:

1. use a root/anchor exchange that leaves `D` and `K` with the root;
2. exploit the originating GG inequalities to contradict aggregate
   underpayment (5);
3. close the equality mode while paying the fixed-priority prefix; or
4. combine incompatible exterior modes through a supplier-closed PI cycle.

Packet arithmetic alone cannot force this: existing exact positive-additive
scalar families realize the corresponding root loss band and underpayment
inequalities.

## Claim boundary

This theorem unifies all typed whole-hit branches once a common-safe root
core is available and eliminates every strictly paying exterior mode. It
does not eliminate (5), close its equality-priority face, normalize every
other retired packet, prove a controlled anchor release under underpayment,
close `T_{L,times}^*`, or prove four-agent/general EFX.
