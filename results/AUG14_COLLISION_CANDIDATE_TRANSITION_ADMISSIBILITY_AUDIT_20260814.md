**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Verdict:** the new set-theoretic donation/isolation constructions are useful
conditional geometries, but three of them do **not** presently establish an
admissible transition of the frozen retired-support state graph. They cannot
be used to claim closure or strict packet progress.

## Governing rule

The frozen contracts impose two independent requirements:

1. a proposed allocation must conserve physical goods, floors, signed
   suppliers, releases, and the current root/anchor obligations; and
2. progress is recorded only after one of the actual `G0--G10` operations
   adopts the tested allocation, response, or anchor coordinate.

Bare feasibility and a smaller intersection `D cap K` do not meet the second
requirement. A response found at a counterfactual allocation cannot decrease
the collision coordinate of the current state unless an admitted operation
reaches that allocation.

## Consequence for the research frontier

The exterior-dominant one/two-source analysis remains a valid theorem **if**
its scalar premise is assumed, but the current campaign has not proved that
every actual collision reaches that premise. The apparent elimination of the
root equality face and the nonroot singleton face is withdrawn.

The smallest honest next gate is therefore an **admission theorem**, not a
new valuation inequality:

> Starting from the exact canonical ledger-free realization `Z^-`, derive an
> already permitted paid splice, enlarged-root sacrifice return, or
> root/orientation reset whose targets have the packet-spread geometry; or
> prove an exact full-premise countermodel to such a derivation.

Until this gate is crossed, dual-anchor response matching and the
three-agent source topology are adversarial diagnostics only. They cannot be
composed into the charge-or-anchor theorem.

## Claim boundary

This audit retracts no verified arithmetic, set partition, threshold, or DAG
census. It retracts only the unsupported inference from a counterfactual test
allocation to an admissible finite-state transition. It does not prove or
refute `T_{L,times}^*`, four-agent EFX, or general additive EFX.
