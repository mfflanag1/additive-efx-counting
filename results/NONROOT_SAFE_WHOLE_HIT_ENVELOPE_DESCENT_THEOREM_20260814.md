# Nonroot safe whole-hit envelope-descent theorem

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Status:** cap-free elimination of every common-safe whole-packet recurrence
with a sacrifice-eligible nonroot holder, including protected-floor and
source-inactive responses

## Input without a current-desire premise

Let `Z` carry a first-priority root `r` strictly above the globally maximal
base. Let nonroot holder `h` own the named packet `K`, and let

\[
 K\subseteq D\subseteq Z_h                     \tag{1}
\]

be the actual protected-floor core for its named source/colliding recipient
`t`, and be common-safe at the actual allocation:

\[
 \tau_i(D)\le v_i(Z_i)\qquad(i\in N).           \tag{2}
\]

No current-desire premise is required. The exact transition must nevertheless
name `t` as the protected source or intended colliding recipient; this
theorem does not assign `D` to an arbitrary owner. If `D` was extracted at
protected floors `F_i<=v_i(Z_i)`, protected-floor safety implies (2), even in
the exact source-inactive debt branch.

## Safe sacrifice move

If the named source is the root, its protected floor is at least the base
root value; a strictly protected-desired `D` is therefore a safe root-anchor
reselection. Otherwise `t` is nonroot. Give `D` to this named recipient,
leave `L=Z_h-D` with `h`, move `Z_t` to charity, and leave the root and the
fourth owner unchanged. This is the existing priority-valid
base-floor/sacrifice handoff, not a new arbitrary assignment. The holder
supplies `D`; `t` supplies its old bundle. Normalize any other retired
support in `Z_t` before execution.

The root remains strictly above base. Therefore an EFX result is a
fixed-priority lexicographic contradiction even if both later owners `h,t`
lose utility. Equality-critical assignment is used only when `t` is the
original intended recipient of the canonical candidate.

## Only the old holder can renew the hit

The only target containing `K` is `D`. By (2), every unchanged observer is
protected against it, and `t` owns it. Thus the only owner who can acquire a
new wall against `D` is the sacrificed old holder `h`.

If that wall exists, globally minimum current-response extraction gives

\[
 E\subsetneq D.                                  \tag{3}
\]

The response is proper because the EFX wall supplies a desired one-good
deletion. If `E cap K` is empty or proper, hitting multiplicity becomes zero
or `K` strictly shrinks. If `K subseteq E`, repeat the same safe sacrifice
with `E`; the current holder and actual response source swap roles, and every owner still
precedes the unchanged strict-root certificate only after `r`.

This produces a strict nested chain of envelopes. It terminates after at
most `|D|-|K|+1` whole hits. At the last envelope, a multi-good `K` can only
produce a proper response, while a singleton `K` is universally safe and
cannot be a wall target. Every response against charity or another target is
disjoint from `K`.

## Consequence by typed branch

- **Strict capture:** no holder compensation is required.
- **Equality-critical capture:** no recipient strictness is required; the
  unchanged root supplies the lexicographic certificate.
- **Base-floor/source-inactive debt:** no currentization is required when the
  holder is nonroot. Safety (2), not source desire, drives the descent.
- **Release:** the moved response and released recipient bundle have their
  actual suppliers; any other retired collision is normalized and named.

Hence the charge-or-anchor theorem has no same-packet nonroot whole-hit
terminal once a common-safe core has been extracted. All remaining direct
whole-hit difficulty is root-held or belongs to a different retired packet
exposed by normalization.

## Claim boundary

This theorem eliminates the named nonroot whole-hit recurrence under the
actual protected-source/colliding-recipient handoff, including its
source-inactive versions. It does not manufacture that handoff when its typed
premises are absent, handle an initial
protected-root holder, prove that normalization of other retired support
closes, eliminate root aggregate underpayment/equality, close all of
`T_{L,times}^*`, or prove four-agent/general EFX.
