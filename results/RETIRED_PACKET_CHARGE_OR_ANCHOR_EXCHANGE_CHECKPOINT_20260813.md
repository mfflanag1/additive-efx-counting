# Retired-packet charge-or-anchor-exchange checkpoint

**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Status:** theorem not yet proved or refuted; cap-free reduction to a
recipient-indivisible packet or protected/root-holder bi-row cut

## Headline boundary

For an exact collision state `T_{L,times}^*`, the recipient-row capture
identity does not by itself pay the current holder.  Direct compensation,
priority-valid sacrifice, protected matching/compression, root reselection,
proper-packet reduction, and normalization reversal now reduce the goal to:

1. a **recipient-indivisible whole packet** when the current holder is a
   later sacrifice-eligible owner;
2. a **root double-atomic bi-row cut**, whose packet lies in the bounded
   root-surplus loss band and whose safe root support is inclusion-minimal
   above the base; or
3. normalization branches that now return, through protected target
   envelopes and ledger-normalized external-star ascent, to that same
   protected cut or strict rank/root progress.

This is not a proof of `T_{L,times}^*`, GG, four-agent EFX, or general EFX,
and it is not a complete-contract counterexample.

## Exact results established at this checkpoint

### Packet accounting and invalid route

For `A=B dot-union K`, strict/equality margin `mu` and deficit `d` satisfy

\[
v_s(K)=\mu+d.
\]

The holder price is independently

\[
p_h(K)=\max\{0,v_h(K)-\sigma_h\}.
\]

Exact positive rational cross-row models satisfy recipient capture while
every named return packet underpays the holder.  These refute only the
capture-implies-payment proof route; they do not refute the GG theorem.

The stronger cap-free parametric family realizes the final bi-row cut for
every packet size, in strict and equality cases, even while every feasible
packet lies strictly inside the root loss band. Thus neither packet
cardinality nor the root-surplus scalar dichotomy can close the final gate
without a structural anchor/external-observer operation.

### Strict whole-packet charge

If some `g in K` has `v_s(g)<mu`, deleting `g` leaves a strict safe candidate
with a proper collision packet.  Hence a recurrent whole-packet survivor is
atomwise critical and obeys

\[
v_s(g)\ge\mu\ (g\in K),
\qquad
(|K|-1)\mu\le d.
\]

In particular `mu>d` forces `K` to be a singleton.  Equality capture
(`mu=0`) remains separately typed.

### Root-facing price duality

For root base `x`, current root surplus `delta`, and response `D`, elementary
root reselection/release closes outside

\[
\delta\le v_r(D)\le x.
\]

Inside this band the exact compensation price is

\[
\pi_r(D)=v_r(D)-\delta,
\qquad
0\le\pi_r(D)\le x-\delta,
\]

which equals the general holder price with root slack `delta`.  Thus the
root-facing branch is a bounded holder-price separator, not a third
independent obstruction.

### Normalization bypass

Against each of the three current owner reservoirs disjoint from a globally
minimum ledger-free response `E`, minimal threshold extraction yields either
a common-safe packet paying that observer against `E`, or a reversed
common-safe desired packet.

If all three reservoirs reverse, include the fourth physical edge by which
the target owner supplies `E` to its source.  Every owner then supplies one
desired packet along a nonself edge.  The four-edge functional graph contains
a directed cycle, and pairwise disjointness makes it supplier-closed PI.
Therefore an exact normalization survivor must contain at least one safe
threshold-payment packet.  Raising the threshold to the complete active-
target envelope makes that packet universal for its observer row. Including
the protected floor gives a two-core/two-hole reduction except in one label
pattern: the payment owner is the response source, both other reversals have
that same source, and the source's whole current bundle is common-safe.  The
survivor is exactly a safe center plus three disjoint safe offers, all desired
by the center. The all-unsafe, source-inactive-leaf, and unsafe-center
recurrences are eliminated.

A final canonical test swaps one leaf's offer to the center and the safe
center bundle back to that leaf.  Success gives two protected cores and
enters two-hole machinery.  Therefore the exact star survivor has the
three-row rejection certificate

\[
v_h(U_s)<\max\{F_h,\tau_h(O_h)\}
\quad\text{for every leaf }h.
\]

The fixed-priority split then routes a nonroot center through its root leaf
to the root-holder price/two-hole branch.  When the center is the root,
canonical normalization first keeps all nonreleasable retired goods at the
root.  An unsafe enlarged core returns to collision routing; if it is safe,
the three-agent EFX theorem allocates the complement and all remaining walls
are precisely the existing one-core/three-hole external-star signs.

Those signs are no longer independent.  A root-to-hole sign yields strict
finite root-value ascent, a distinct nonroot response, or retired collision.
Once root-to-hole signs are exhausted, a hole-to-root sign gives a safe
proper response inside the root core and hence root reselection, controlled
release, retired collision, or the bounded root-holder price band.

## Smallest remaining theorem

The next load-bearing statement is now the **indivisible-packet/protected-
cut-to-anchor lemma**. Starting from either atomwise whole-packet capture or
the bounded root-facing price band,
it must prove one of:

1. a physically admissible packet paying the losing holder/observer;
2. a supplier-closed desired cycle and hence PI;
3. a strict source/target-rank or proper-retired-packet reduction;
4. a root/anchor operation; or
5. a contradiction to global lexicographic maximality or a typed floor.

The target envelope and external-star ascent remove the independent
normalization recurrence. The remaining difficulty is genuinely cross-row:
recipient capture and root surplus still do not force the holder-price
inequality.

The complete-pool threshold reversal removes failure of a particular return
menu: when a compatible mode reaches the price, a minimal crossing packet
either pays safely or exposes a genuine safe response. The full-packet
survivor therefore satisfies

\[
\max_{R\in\mathfrak R_h}v_h(R)<p_h
\]

over the complete family of downward-closed compatible release modes. The
bi-row split test sharpens this further. Any proper `T` paying both the
recipient and holder floors physically separates `T` from `K-T`, so a later
single-target response can hit only a proper collision support. A protected
holder can therefore survive only with the exact certificate

\[
v_s(T)\ge d\Longrightarrow v_h(T)>\sigma+M_h(T)
\qquad(T\subseteq K).
\]

For a later sacrifice-eligible holder, its auxiliary floor need not be paid:
any proper recipient-feasible split already routes to proper collision
support, PI/rank progress, or the protected/root gate. Hence the exact final
alternatives are (i) a recipient-indivisible whole packet in the nonroot
branch and (ii) the displayed bi-row cut for a protected/root holder. Root
support minimization further shows that the latter root core is atomwise
base-critical: every root good is worth at least its surplus and
`(|A_r|-1)delta<=x`. A final
proof must convert these into anchor change or contradiction; a refutation
must realize them inside the full globally maximal recurrent GG contract.

The cut itself has a cap-free positive-additive scalar realization for every
packet size, in strict and equality cases. This rules out a purely row-local
or cardinality-potential proof but is not a GG counterexample.
