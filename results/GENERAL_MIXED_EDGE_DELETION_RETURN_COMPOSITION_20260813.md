# General mixed-edge deletion-return composition

**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Status:** cap-free re-run for every genuine mixed Hamiltonian matching;
the exact survivor is a decorated-core-pool compensation separator

## Input

Let the closing response source in `E subseteq Y_t` be `s`. Let

\[
 h_0=t\to h_1\to h_2\to h_3\to c                \tag{1}
\]

be a PI-free rank-four matching at one exact paid state `Y`.  Its selected
core for source `i` is `K_i`.  Let `C subseteq N` be the decorated sources:

\[
 \begin{cases}
 v_i(K_i)>v_i(Y_i),\quad
 \tau_h(K_i)\le v_h(Y_h),&i\in C,\\
 v_i(K_i)>v_i(X_i),\quad
 \tau_h(K_i)\le v_h(X_h),&i\notin C.
 \end{cases}                                      \tag{2}

The genuine mixed branch has `1<=|C|<=3`.  A distinct current-safe response
`E subseteq Y_t` enters the unmatched hole.  Put `H=Y_t\setminus E`, assume

\[
 v_t(K_t+H)<\tau_t(E),                            \tag{3}

choose a least-`t` good `g in E`, and let `P=E-g`.

## Atomic deletion-return allocation

Allocate

\[
 Q_t=P,
 \qquad Q_i=K_i\quad(i\ne t).                    \tag{4}

The unused selected core `K_t`, `H`, `g`, and every unselected target
residual remain in charity.  Target disjointness proves physical
disjointness.  Equation (3) gives

\[
 v_t(P)=\tau_t(E)>v_t(K_t+H)>v_t(X_t),            \tag{5}

while (2) pays every other owner strictly above base.  Thus (4) is a
strict Pareto candidate for every value of `|C|`; no selected core is
misclassified as old.

## Exact decorated-debt matrix

The allocated special targets are

\[
 \mathcal D=\{P\}\cup\{K_i:i\in C\setminus\{t\}\}. \tag{6}

All other allocated cores are base-common-safe.  Put

\[
 \mu_h=v_h(Q_h)-v_h(X_h)>0,                       \tag{7}
\]

and

\[
 \Gamma_h=\max_{Z\in\mathcal D,\,Z\ne Q_h}
       (\tau_h(Z)-v_h(X_h))_+.                   \tag{8}

Then (4) is EFX **if and only if**

\[
 \mu_h\ge\Gamma_h\qquad(h\in N).              \tag{9}

This is the complete observer budget.  It has one column for the closing
remainder `P` and one column for every allocated decorated matching core.

Every decorated recipient `i in C\setminus{t}` automatically satisfies
(9): her own current-desired core is worth more than `Y_i`, while every
column of (8) is current-safe and hence at most `Y_i`.  Therefore only:

1. the hole owner `t`, who receives `P`; and
2. sources receiving old cores

can be underfunded.  This is the exact general replacement for the former
“mixed-margin nonowner” classification.

## Old-recipient residual/rotation branch

Let old-core recipient `h!=t` fail (9).  Its current target is

\[
 Y_h=C_h\mathbin{\dot\cup}R_h,                   \tag{10}

where `C_h` is the incoming selected core.  Since every special target is
current-safe, `Gamma_h+v_h(X_h)<=v_h(Y_h)`.

- If `K_h+R_h` pays (9), attach `R_h`.  An unsafe enlarged target yields a
  globally minimal decorated response with its complete new target label.
- If it still fails, cancellation gives

  \[
  v_h(C_h)>v_h(K_h)>v_h(X_h).                    \tag{11}
  \]

  An old `C_h` is a base-safe replacement edge.  A decorated `C_h` gains a
  new source at the new physical target.  Either case is an explicit
  alternating core pivot; internal supply must be checked from the signed
  release record.

No other old-recipient scalar separator survives.

## Selected-core/hole-pool selector

After every old recipient is paid or has produced the response/pivot above,
the remaining direct separator is a hole row

\[
 v_t(P)<\max_{i\in C\setminus\{t\}}\tau_t(K_i).  \tag{12}

Let

\[
 R=\{t,s\}\cup C,
 \qquad
 \mathcal P=Y_t\mathbin{\dot\cup}
             \mathop{\dot\bigcup}_{i\in R}K_i.    \tag{13}

Thus the selector includes the complete hole target `Y_t=P+g+H`, the selected
core of the hole owner, the selected core of the closing response source,
and every decorated selected core. Globally partition `mathcal P` among the
distinct participant sources `R` and charity, keeping every old source
outside `R` on its selected old core (or on a fixed paid compensation bundle
from the preceding branch). A partition is feasible when:

1. every recipient of the pool remains strictly above the priority-valid
   base floor;
2. the pool recipients are mutually EFX; and
3. every fixed old recipient is safe against every pool cell.

Any feasible partition, together with the old cores, is an EFX
lexicographic improvement.  This is a finite cap-free selector over the
entire special pool, not a fixed packet menu.

Failure is the exact **selected-core/hole-pool compensation separator**: no
redistribution of the complete hole target and every selected core belonging
to a hole/closing/decorated participant can
simultaneously preserve the decorated sources' strict floors and pay every
observer debt. For `|C|=1`, the pool already includes `Y_t`, `K_t`, the
closing source's core, and the unique decorated source's core, with overlaps
removed when roles coincide. The direct two-core exchange of the corrected
one-decorated theorem is therefore always a strict subfamily. For
`|C|=2,3`, it is the previously omitted multi-decorated branch. More
precisely, the smaller direct selector is only a subfamily: its local
countermodel has 89 feasible allocations once `g` and `K_t` are restored.

The audit also gives a separate positive-rational local separator for the
complete pool (13). Every pool cell that pays the decorated source contains
at least three pool goods; an old-core observer values every such deletion
remainder above her selected-core margin. The decorated cores are nevertheless
current-safe because that observer has larger realized surplus at `Y`.
All 262,144 pool partitions fail. Thus current safety alone cannot force the
selector: the next operation must retain or recruit the signed component
that realizes the blocking observer's surplus. This is a local interface
countermodel, not a globally maximal GG state.

## Exact result

Every mixed Hamiltonian deletion-return state now has one of four outcomes:

1. atomic EFX/Pareto improvement by (9);
2. a paid old-recipient residual whose unsafe target yields a fully labeled
   current response;
3. an incoming-core alternating pivot/source change by (11); or
4. a feasible special-pool EFX allocation, or the selected-core/hole-pool
   compensation separator.

The old four-common-core shortcut and the old rank-completing splice are
both superseded.  The remaining flower-specific task is to contradict the
pool separator using the root/q reservoir and signed component inequalities,
or realize it at a globally maximal GG state.

## Claim boundary

This theorem re-runs payment and observer safety for `|C|=1,2,3` without
assuming four old cores.  It does not force a feasible pool partition,
terminate an exact repeated response created by an unsafe residual target,
derive the GG root/q reverse inequality, close GG, or prove four-agent/
general additive EFX.
