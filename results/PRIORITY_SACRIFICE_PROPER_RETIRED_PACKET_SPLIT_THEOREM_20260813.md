# Priority-sacrifice proper retired-packet split theorem

**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Status:** cap-free elimination of the nonroot holder-price gate as an
independent terminal for every proper recipient-feasible split

## Exact input

Use an exact `T_{L,times}^*` candidate

\[
A=B\mathbin{\dot\cup}K,
\qquad
d=F_s-v_s(B)>0,                                  \tag{1}
\]

with current holder `h`. Assume:

1. `h` is not the protected first-priority root and is the designated later
   owner that the existing sacrifice handoff may leave below its auxiliary
   floor;
2. the earlier root bundle and its strict-above-base certificate are
   unchanged when a subpacket of `K` moves from `h` to `s`;
3. every non-`K` transfer and retained protected core of the canonical
   candidate is unchanged; and
4. `A` is safe at the exact tested floor vector, so every subset `B+T` is
   safe by deletion monotonicity.

These are the precise priority-valid sacrifice premises. The theorem does
not waive the root floor or silently sacrifice a protected earlier owner.

## Theorem

If a nonempty proper subpacket satisfies

\[
v_s(T)\ge d,
\qquad
\varnothing\ne T\subsetneq K,                   \tag{2}
\]

form the partial candidate that gives `B+T` to `s`, leaves `K-T` with `h`,
and performs the unchanged non-`K` transfers. Do not require an auxiliary
floor for the designated sacrificed holder.

Then the candidate has either one of the permitted outcomes:

1. it is EFX and lexicographically improves the globally maximal base,
   because the earlier root remains strict;
2. a release-compatible desired cycle is PI;
3. an outside supplier or new source/target strictly improves the finite
   component/rank coordinate;
4. an extracted response meeting retired support has collision packet a
   proper subset of `K`; or
or it transfers the obligation to the already isolated protected/root gate:

5. the ledger-free response enters the already proved
   normalization/external-star routing, which returns to 1--4 or a typed
   root/protected-holder price gate.

In particular, no holder compensation inequality is an entry premise for
this branch.

## Proof

Equation (2) gives

\[
v_s(B+T)-F_s=v_s(T)-d\ge0.                       \tag{3}
\]

The earlier strict root and every nonsacrificed protected assignment are
unchanged. Feasibility and supplier provenance are explicit: the packet
edge is `h -> s`, its supplier `h` participates, and no good is duplicated.
If the resulting allocation is EFX, the unchanged strict root gives the
lexicographic contradiction even when `h` is below its auxiliary floor.

Otherwise perform the complete comparison and release audit. At the tested
allocation,

\[
K\cap(B+T)=T,
\qquad
K\cap(Y_h-T)=K-T,                                \tag{4}
\]

and every other target is disjoint from `K`. A globally minimum response is
contained in one target. Hence every retired intersection is contained in
`T` or `K-T`; both are proper subsets of `K`. A supplied desired cycle is PI,
and an outside supplier or a new response coordinate is existing finite
progress. A ledger-free exact response is precisely the already typed
normalization-bypass input, not a new terminal. Its proved reservoir,
target-envelope, priority, and external-star routing ends in progress, PI,
or the root/protected-holder gate. This exhausts the complete audit.

## Consequences by typed branch

### Strict capture

For a sacrifice-eligible holder, a recurrent strict-capture state can have
no proper recipient-feasible packet. Therefore every `g in K` satisfies

\[
v_s(K-g)<d,
\qquad
v_s(g)>\mu,
\qquad
(|K|-1)\mu<d.                                   \tag{5}
\]

Thus `mu>=d` forces `K` to be a singleton. The weak atomwise inequality from
the compensated whole-packet analysis is sharpened to strict recipient
indivisibility in this priority branch.

### Equality critical

When `mu=0`, positivity already gives `v_s(T)<d` for every proper `T`. Hence
the theorem explains exactly why packet splitting is unavailable: equality
survives only as a whole-packet priority/anchor problem.

### Base-floor debt

Only the named later holder floor is waived. A debt belonging to the root or
another protected earlier row is not covered and remains in the protected
holder bi-row branch.

### Release compatibility

The direct packet edge is supplier-closed by including `h`. Any additional
release edge keeps its signed supplier label and is processed before PI is
declared. The target-locality conclusion (4) does not depend on assigning an
incompatible compensation packet.

## Claim boundary

This theorem removes the sacrifice-eligible holder's own price as an
independent terminal for every proper recipient-feasible split. It may
transfer a ledger-free external-star wall to the already isolated
root/protected-holder gate. It does not make the first-priority root
sacrifice-eligible, resolve a protected earlier-owner debt, create a proper
recipient-feasible packet in the equality/indivisible branch, eliminate the
root/protected-holder bi-row cut, close `T_{L,times}^*`, or prove
four-agent/general EFX.
