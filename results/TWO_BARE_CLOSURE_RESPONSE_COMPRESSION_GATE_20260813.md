# Two-bare closure response-compression gate

**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Status:** exploratory provenance subcase only; the claimed universal
two-bare reduction was withdrawn

## Input forced by the preceding reductions

At a saturated root-facing rank-three state, failed four-edge surplus-star
payment produces captured base-`d` support. Close the signed base-support
graph from `d` and reassemble the resulting owner set `S`. The reassembly is
complete and pays every owner. The exploratory subcase below additionally
assumes at least two closure owners are bare:

\[
 E_i=Y_i\cap\left(P_X\mathbin{\dot\cup}
                  \mathop{\dot\bigcup}_{a\notin S}X_a\right)=\varnothing.
                                                               \tag{1}
\]

This assumption is not yet derived from PI-freeness. The attempted derivation
failed because an owner-minimal desired subset need not be common-safe for
other observers. Conditional on the assumption, with four owners and `d in
S`, the only proper-closure cardinalities are:

1. `|S|=2`, in which case both closure owners are bare; or
2. `|S|=3`, in which case at least two of its three owners are bare.

No corresponding unconditional statement is currently known for `S=N`.

## Exact missing implication

Let `Z` be this paid reassembly. Every non-EFX failure is converted into a
globally inclusion-minimal common-safe desired response. Saturation records
its source, target, desired core, released packet, supplier, and signed
observer budget at the identical physical state.

Prove that the complete response relation of a two-bare closed component has
one of the following outcomes:

1. a matching of rank `|S|`, so saturated represented-component charity
   extraction applies;
2. an internally supplied response cycle/self-response, hence PI;
3. a source or supplier outside `S`, hence strict component growth;
4. a new core/release/safety label or strict same-record source progress;
5. a complete EFX allocation using at most four support-provenance packet
   edges.

If none occurs, the countermodel must have a maximum response matching of
rank at most `|S|-1` with at least two bare unmatched owners, while satisfying
every full signed-state and saturation exclusion. Hall compression localizes
all remaining walls to the unmatched residual bundles; the problem is a
two-residual response-rank defect, not donor payment.

## Why this is the right next decision problem

The earlier full-capture atomization has two externally retained base-charity
goods, leaving only one bare closure owner. That fact does not exclude it.
The two-bare model is nevertheless a useful conditional adversary and must
atomize:

- a closed two- or three-owner signed-support component;
- at least two bare closure owners;
- arbitrary internal displaced-support packets;
- one outside paid owner when the closure is proper;
- the complete ordinary/current decorated response relation; and
- the actual saturation exclusions, rather than only response rank three.

SAT must be audited against all of those premises and every unrestricted
four-edge allocation. UNSAT is only a bounded-atom theorem, from which the
shortest Hall/observer inequality should be extracted.

## Claim boundary

This checkpoint does not prove the missing implication or remove the
full-capture cell. It freezes a conditional provenance variant for testing
whether the harder two-bare case itself admits a four-edge obstruction.
