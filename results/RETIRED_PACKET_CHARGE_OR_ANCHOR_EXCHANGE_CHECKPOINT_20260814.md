**Date:** 2026-08-14

## Headline

The main new result is an admissible exact-state reduction, not a
counterfactual allocation search. In the canonical ledger-free state, a
current response containing the whole retired packet must be a proper subset
of the holder's target. Its omitted residual is nonempty and disjoint from
the packet. Routing whole current bundles around every possible supplier
cycle either clears the named packet from the only new target or produces a
finite explicit Hall cut.

This is real narrowing of `T_{L,times}^*`, but it is not EFX closure.

## Verified actual-state lemmas

### Whole-hit localization

Write the canonical holder target as

\[
 Z_h=D\mathbin{\dot\cup}L,
 \qquad K\subseteq D.
\]

For a globally minimum current response:

- the response source `t` is distinct from `h`;
- `L` is nonempty and disjoint from `K`; and
- every good of `D`, including each retired atom, is critical in the source
  row at the response margin.

### Bilateral charge

Give `D` to `t` and `L+Z_t` to `h`. This is a physical bilateral exchange.
It weakly pays the holder exactly when

\[
 v_h(Z_t)\ge v_h(D).
\]

On success, every new wall is against the `K`-free target `L+Z_t`. On
failure, the exact charge is

\[
 v_h(Z_t)<v_h(D).
\]

### Retirement-supplier cycle

For retirement supplier `u` distinct from `h,t`, the cycle

\[
 h\to t:D,
 \qquad t\to u:Z_t,
 \qquad u\to h:Z_u
\]

pays exactly when `u` accepts `Z_t` and `h` accepts `Z_u` against `D`.
Failure gives the two-row disjunction

\[
 v_u(Z_t)<v_u(Z_u)
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 v_h(Z_u)<v_h(D).
\]

### Complete four-owner path Hall cut

Let `a,b` be the other two owners. The five simple compensation paths are

\[
 th,\quad tah,\quad tbh,\quad tabh,\quad tbah.
\]

Every paid path is a supplier-closed cycle and leaves only a new `K`-free
holder target. Exact Boolean exhaustion proves that failure of all five is
equivalent to inequalities (7)--(11) of
`FOUR_OWNER_CURRENT_BUNDLE_COMPENSATION_PATH_HALL_THEOREM_20260814.md`.
This exhausts all compensation routes that move only whole current bundles.

## Current exact frontier

The remaining theorem now has four genuinely different gates:

1. **current-response path-Hall cut:** use the GG champion/floor structure to
   force one of the five paths, or split an old compensation bundle;
2. **protected/base-floor response:** upgrade the source to its actual
   current utility before applying the path theorem, or carry the exact
   observer debt through an anchor exchange;
3. **other retired support:** normalize old bundles used on a compensation
   path without replacing one collision by an untyped one; and
4. **exact normalization bypass:** a paid path can still return a previously
   recorded ledger-free response, which needs rank/root progress or a final
   anchor change.

The conditional donation geometries are not a fifth theorem branch; they are
possible escape templates awaiting an admission proof.
