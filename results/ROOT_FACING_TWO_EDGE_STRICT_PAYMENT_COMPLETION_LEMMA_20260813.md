# Root-facing two-edge strict-payment completion lemma

**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Status:** exact named-provenance theorem for the declared packet cell;
packet cardinalities may be arbitrary, but two structural hypotheses are not
yet derived from the universal GG state

**Statement-integrity correction:** the first version described `Q_0` as
part of every saturated state.  The earlier GG lemmas guarantee only
`K subseteq Q`; equality `K=Q` is possible for an inclusion-minimal restoring
packet.  Everything below is exact under `Q_0=Q\K nonempty`.  Moreover, the
cancellation uses the named provenance identity (3).  The general GG
selector does not assert that `K\J` is exactly old `d`-support.  Thus both
proper residual and provenance alignment are explicit hypotheses, not
universal consequences.  The general case must use the captured-own-support
decomposition of the two-hole residual-split lemma.

## Named packet state

At a root-facing fixed-`U` state in the proper-residual branch, write the physical support
allocation as

\[
 Y_r=A\mathbin{\dot\cup}H,
 \quad Y_q=Q,
 \quad Y_d=W,
 \quad Y_s=E,
 \quad P=\{p\}.                                   \tag{1}
\]

The three represented cores satisfy

\[
 v_r(A)>v_r(X_r),\qquad
 v_q(Q)>v_q(X_q),\qquad
 v_s(E)>v_s(X_s).                                 \tag{2}
\]

Use the named `d`-restoration decomposition

\[
 X_d=H\mathbin{\dot\cup}(K\setminus J)
          \mathbin{\dot\cup}G_q                 \tag{3}
\]

and

\[
 Q=J\mathbin{\dot\cup}(K\setminus J)
       \mathbin{\dot\cup}Q_0,                   \tag{4}
\]

where `Q_0` is nonempty.  The restoration core and surviving safe-wall
inequalities are

\[
 v_d(J+(K\setminus J)+H)>v_d(X_d),               \tag{5}
\]

\[
 v_d(W)\ge \tau_d(Q).                             \tag{6}
\]

All packets are arbitrary finite sets of positive goods.

## Sacrificed-owner payment

Substituting (3) into (5) cancels the common packets and gives

\[
 v_d(J)>v_d(G_q).                                 \tag{7}
\]

Let `g` be a least-`d` good of `Q`.  Since `Q_0` is nonempty and values are
positive,

\[
 v_d(Q_0)\ge v_d(g).
\]

Therefore

\[
 \tau_d(Q)=v_d(Q)-v_d(g)
 \ge v_d(J)+v_d(K\setminus J).                   \tag{8}
\]

Combining (6)--(8),

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 v_d(W+H)
 &\ge v_d(J)+v_d(K\setminus J)+v_d(H)\\
 &>v_d(G_q)+v_d(K\setminus J)+v_d(H)\\
 &=v_d(X_d).                                      \tag{9}
 \end{aligned}
\]

Thus the sacrificed owner is not merely weakly restored: `W+H` strictly
pays `d`.

## Fixed complete allocation

Allocate

\[
 Z_r=A,
 \qquad Z_q=Q,
 \qquad Z_d=W+H,
 \qquad Z_s=E+\{p\},
 \qquad P'=\varnothing.                           \tag{10}
\]

This is a partition of every good.  Relative to `Y`, it uses exactly two
functional support edges,

\[
 r\to d,qquad \mathrm{charity}\to s.             \tag{11}
\]

Equations (2) and (9), plus positivity of `p`, show

\[
 v_i(Z_i)>v_i(X_i)\qquad(i=r,q,d,s).              \tag{12}
\]

No packet-size or value cap is used after assuming (3)--(6), but (3) and
nonemptiness of `Q_0` are explicit structural hypotheses.  In the
twelve-atom model they are represented by the provenance of `h,k,gq` and the
positive atom `q0`; neither may be silently promoted to the general GG state.

## Consequence for the four-edge theorem

In this named-provenance proper-residual branch, the payment layer is closed before any R2/R3 topology choice: the fixed
two-edge candidate (10) strictly pays every owner.  If it is EFX, it proves
the desired complete allocation with slack in the edge bound.  If it is not
EFX, the paid-splice observer-failure lemma produces a globally minimal
current-safe decorated response with its complete signed compensation state.

Within that branch, the remaining step is:

> exclude exact recurrence of the decorated response generated by the fixed
> paid candidate (10), using the saturated exits or the universal full-owner
> charity-exchange extraction theorem.

The full universal theorem additionally requires returning the actual
captured-own-support packet from the selected cores.  `K=Q` is one extreme,
but provenance misalignment can occur even when `K` is proper.  The two-hole
capture inequality and the q-budget/fourth-owner compensation selectors are
the relevant general machinery.

## Verification and boundary

functional graph, empty charity, and all four strict-payment implications.

This lemma does not derive (3), derive `Q\K nonempty`, return arbitrary
captured own support, handle `K=Q`, prove (10) is EFX, eliminate the resulting
decorated-response recurrence, prove the four-edge theorem, or prove general
additive EFX.
