## Input and operation

Let `X` be the globally maximal EFX-with-charity base with first-priority
root `r`, and let

\[
 O=G\setminus X_r.
\]

Assume the named root-held retired packet is nonempty and base-contained:

\[
 K\subseteq X_r.
\]

Choose `g in K` and a named nonroot owner `a`. Test the complete physical
allocation

\[
 Z_r=G-g=(X_r-g)\mathbin{\dot\cup}O,
 \qquad Z_a=\{g\},                              \tag{1}
\]

with the other two nonroot bundles and charity empty. Every current holder
of an exterior good is an explicit supplier to the root; every nonroot owner
is later than `r` and may be sacrificed once the root is strict. If an
exterior good carries another retirement label, its current holder and
collision are appended to the exact signed record. No PI edge is inferred.

This is an explicit global supplier-star sacrifice operation. It is not
justified by pretending that all goods have silently returned to their base
cells.

## Strict branch

The root margin over the base is

\[
 v_r(Z_r)-v_r(X_r)=v_r(O)-v_r(g).               \tag{2}
\]

Suppose

\[
 v_r(O)>v_r(g).                                  \tag{3}
\]

If (1) is EFX, (3) contradicts global lexicographic maximality. Otherwise
every EFX wall is against the root target:

- the singleton `{g}` cannot be strongly envied because its one-good
  deletion is empty;
- an empty target cannot be strongly envied; and
- the root owns her own target.

Extract a globally minimum response inside `G-g`. Its intersection with the
named packet satisfies

\[
 D\cap K\subseteq K-g\subsetneq K.             \tag{4}
\]

Thus every failed EFX test gives an actual proper/zero packet response.
Adopting the tested allocation/response is strict reduction of the named
packet coordinate. Any simultaneously moved second retired label is
recorded, but it does not restore `g` to this response.

## Exact survivor

If no atom satisfies (3), bundle nondegeneracy excludes equality between the
physically distinct packets `O` and `{g}`. Hence

\[
 v_r(O)<v_r(g)
 \qquad(g\in K).                                \tag{5}
\]

Consequently

\[
 |K|v_r(O)<v_r(K).                              \tag{6}
\]

This is the **base-contained exterior-dominance face**. Because `O`
contains every good outside the base root bundle, it is stronger than
underpayment by any one local compensation reservoir.

## Claim boundary

This theorem admits the strict global pooling operation and proves (5)--(6).
It does not contradict exterior dominance, prove a global decrease when a
proper `K` response simultaneously exposes a different retired packet,
close the multi-label charge recurrence, `T_{L,times}^*`, GG, four-agent EFX,
or general additive EFX.
