## Frozen interface

Let `X` be the positive, bundle-nondegenerate, globally fixed-priority
lexicographically maximal one-charity `GG` base.  The selected mutual-cycle
root `r` has an old common-safe desired core

\[
 D_r=T_q\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{f\}
 \subseteq G\setminus X_r,
 \qquad v_r(D_r)>v_r(X_r).                       \tag{1}
\]

This is the permanent old edge `r -> q` retained by the anchored
`G0--G10` branch.  A transition that changes the proof root or its physical
anchor label is already the contracted root/anchor-exchange outcome; at an
exact fixed-label `T_{L,times}^*` recurrence, (1) remains part of the signed
state.

Put

\[
 x=v_r(X_r),
 \qquad O=G\setminus X_r.                       \tag{2}
\]

## Contradiction

Suppose the root holds a nonempty retired packet `K` and all previously
admitted packet-reduction, EFX, PI, and anchor-exchange outcomes fail.  The
global-exterior sacrifice theorem and its base-containment consequence then
give

\[
 \varnothing\ne K\subseteq X_r,
 \qquad v_r(k)>v_r(O)\quad(k\in K).              \tag{3}
\]

Choose any `k in K`.  Positivity, (1), and the two support inclusions give

\[
 v_r(k)\le v_r(X_r)=x
 <v_r(D_r)\le v_r(O).                            \tag{4}
\]

Equations (3) and (4) are contradictory.  Equivalently,

\[
 v_r(k)>v_r(O)\ge v_r(D_r)>x\ge v_r(k).
\]

Therefore the strict complete-current-exterior dominance terminal is empty
inside the frozen anchored `GG` contract.  Every root-held collision reaches
one of the earlier admitted outcomes before that cut can recur.

## Why the old edge is admissible evidence

No transfer along `r -> q` is executed in this proof.  Only its base-row
preference inequality and support are used.  Consequently the argument does
not require the old core to remain current-desired after later owners move,
does not infer historical reversibility, and does not spend the charity good
twice.  The complete signed state retains the old edge precisely as a base-
desired/base-common-safe anchor; changing to a label without that anchored
edge is a physical root/anchor transition rather than the same recurrence.

## Typed consequences

- **Strict capture:** a strictly root-paying subpacket already reduces the
  packet; universal failure would imply (3), contradicted by (4).
- **Equality-critical payment:** equality is converted to a strict
  packet-spread operation before (3), so it cannot survive separately.
- **Base-floor/source-inactive debt:** the global supplier-star sacrifice
  waives only later-owner floors while retaining the first-priority root;
  the contradiction uses no nonroot payment premise.
- **Release compatibility:** the contradiction is valuation-only after the
  supplier-star operation has been admitted.  It proposes no additional
  transfer and therefore creates no new release obligation.

## Claim boundary

This theorem eliminates the root-held `T_{L,times}^*` survivor under the
frozen anchored `GG` interface.  It does not eliminate the parked-support
class `T_{L,perp}^*`, the empty-ledger class `T_0^*`, the other seven
three-nonsingleton cycle classes, or prove four-agent/general additive EFX.
The later charity-reset, two-source-debt, and forced-`GGS` analyses are valid
conditional reductions, but their full-premise root face is empty once (1)
is composed with exterior dominance.
